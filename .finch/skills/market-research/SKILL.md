# Market Research Skill

When assessing an asset:
1. Separate sourced observations from assumptions and forecasts.
2. Record quote source and timestamp. Never describe demo data as live.
3. Review price momentum, liquidity, valuation context, earnings, sector catalysts and macro sensitivity when data is available.
4. List at least three thesis invalidation conditions.
5. If live headlines or filings are unavailable, state the limitation and request verification.
6. Do not issue personalized investment advice or guarantee returns.
